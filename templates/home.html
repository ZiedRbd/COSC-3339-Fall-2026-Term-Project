What happens to a ticket

  1. It goes to whoever is on call

    Each team keeps a solver on call around the clock. Your ticket is theirs the moment you send it, and they are told in the app and by email.

  2. If nobody starts, it moves up

    Every team sets how long a ticket may wait. When the time is up it goes to the next person on the team's list, and sooner when the problem is urgent.

  3. You hear back twice

    Once when work starts and once when it is fixed. Every step in between is on the ticket's timeline.
